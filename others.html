Story 01 · Biking

Technology on two wheels

Hobby

Biking has always been about movement, freedom, and seeing more of the world around us. An e-bike adds another layer: it turns the bicycle into a thoughtful hardware–software system you can ride.

What interests me is not replacing the work of pedaling, but how well-integrated technology can make a ride more accessible and useful. A responsive motor and torque sensor can amplify effort; ride data can make distance and energy visible; and integrated lights, displays, and connected features can make everyday trips feel safer and more intuitive.

For anyone who enjoys both cycling and the engineering behind the experience, an e-bike is a fascinating meeting point between mechanics, electronics, software, and the open road.

What makes it interesting

  • 01Responsive assistance that works with your pedaling
  • 02Ride intelligence through displays and app connectivity
  • 03Integrated safety with lights and turn signals
  • 04Longer possibilities for commutes and weekend exploration
